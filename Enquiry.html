About Trailblazer Esports Arena

Owned & Managed by: Jeffery Yelnats

Established: August 2017

Mission

To deliver a premium esports arena experience by providing accessible arena access, professional training, and competitive opportunities that grow the local gaming community.

Vision

To become South Africa's leading esports destination, where players of all levels can train, compete, connect, and grow within a vibrant gaming community.That believes in the power of gaming when people come together for shared experiences.

Overview

Trailblazer Esports Arena offers arena access, esports training, day passes, monthly memberships with merch, and tournament endorsement pathways for Platinum members. This website redesign project aims to present these offerings clearly with a modern, responsive design. The Gaming community is based on fast paced gameplay and the excellence of creativity in spaces that build collaboration and teamwork

Goals & Objectives

  • Improve user experience and navigation across devices.
  • Make e-commerce and membership sign-ups simple and secure.
  • Communicate value and build brand identity to encourage repeat visits.
